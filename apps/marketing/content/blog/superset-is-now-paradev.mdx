Earlier this month we said Superset would get a new name. Here it is: Superset is now **Paradev**. Our new home is [paradev.com](https://paradev.com).

Only the name changes. The app, your account, your workspaces and the team behind them are the same.

## Why a new name

Many companies and projects are called Superset. We wanted a name that's truly ours, to build a lasting company on. Paradev is that name.

## Where we are

In under a year, more than 145,000 developers in 180 countries have run Superset and created over 1.85 million agent workspaces, and one in eight Fortune 500 companies have used it. The repo has more than 15,000 GitHub stars. The app now speaks 17 languages.

Engineers at Netflix, Microsoft, NVIDIA, DoorDash, Wix and Mistral AI use it, and in Asia, engineers at ByteDance, Toss, Kakao, Grab, LINE and Rakuten.

## What's next

Today one engineer runs dozens of agents in parallel in Paradev, each in its own Git worktree, on their own machine and subscription. Next, we want every engineering team to have its own software factory: agents that start work on a schedule or an event, run in parallel, and leave reviewed changes ready to merge in the morning. Our most advanced users already work this way.

We closed our $11.5M seed round this summer, led by USV with Y Combinator and Paul Graham, to build it. And we're hiring: [paradev.com/careers](https://paradev.com/careers).

## What changes

- **The name.** Superset is now Paradev. You'll see it in the desktop app, the iPhone app, the docs and our emails.
- **The website.** [paradev.com](https://paradev.com), with docs at [docs.paradev.com](https://docs.paradev.com). Every superset.sh link redirects to the same page on paradev.com.
- **Our accounts.** X [@paradevai](https://x.com/paradevai), LinkedIn [/company/paradevai](https://www.linkedin.com/company/paradevai), YouTube [@paradevai](https://www.youtube.com/@paradevai). Same [Discord](https://discord.gg/cZeD9WYcV7) server.
- **Emails.** For a few more weeks, emails still come from superset.sh, with the sender name "Paradev (formerly Superset)". Then they move to paradev.com.
- **Card statements.** Charges show as PARADEV.

## What doesn't change

- The product, its features and the team.
- Your account, workspaces, settings, agents, automations and data. You stay signed in.
- Pricing and billing: same plans, same prices.
- Your links and integrations, such as GitHub, Slack and Linear. A sign-in or consent screen may show the new name.
- The `superset` command in your terminal and the Homebrew tap.

The desktop app updates to the new name on its own. There's nothing to reinstall.

Paradev (formerly Superset) is the self-improving code editor. Run Claude Code, Codex and any other coding agent in parallel. [Download it here](https://paradev.com/download).

Thanks for building with us :)

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to do anything?

No. Your account, workspaces, settings and billing carry over.

### Will my links break?

No. superset.sh links redirect to the same page on paradev.com.

### Do I need to reinstall the app?

No. The desktop app updates to the new name on its own.

### Does the CLI change?

No. The `superset` command and `brew install superset-sh/tap/superset` keep working.

### Where do emails come from now?

For a few more weeks, still superset.sh, with the sender "Paradev (formerly Superset)". Then paradev.com.

### Why does my card statement say PARADEV?

That's us. Same plan, same price.

### Is Paradev related to Apache Superset?

No. Neither was Superset.

### Is it the same company?

Yes. Same team, and your contracts and invoices stay valid. Paradev is a product of Superset Inc.
